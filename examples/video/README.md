# The video brain

Robin's fictional brain for the hackathon video, used by `scripts/video-reset.ts`. Robin is the
President of Kestrel Labs, a made-up company, and shares parts of their knowledge base with the
exec team's assistants; nothing here is real. Only the sample's `rules/` (checks, RULES.md)
come from `examples/brain`; the notes are all here. Dates are written as `@D+n@` (n days from
the day the reset runs) so the week on screen is always this week.

- `rules/policy.yaml`: Maya's claude.ai (VP Strategy: strategy and board prep), Theo's Claude Code
  (VP People: org and hiring), Priya's Claude Code (CTO: the technical roadmap), Alice (new VP
  of Partnerships, her own sign-in: the Q4 launch only), the gardener, and the steward.
- `brain/`: Robin's notebook. Meeting notes (exec staff, 1:1s with Maya, Theo and Priya: date, attendees, what was decided, follow-ups), board prep, the FY27 plan, the Q4 launch, the VP Sales search, the reorg, the
  platform roadmap, three people; private: Robin's board feedback (what "ask to see" asks for),
  a personnel concern, the acquisition talks (area `deals`, never offered) and executive compensation (money, never
  shared or offered).
- `gardener-night.yaml` is what the gardener proposes in the night the reset replays.
