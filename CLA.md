## How to sign

Comment on your first pull request with:

> I have read the pocketBrain Individual CLA (CLA.md at commit `<sha>`) and I agree to its terms for this and all my future contributions. — `<full name>`, `<GitHub login>`, `<date>`

A CLA bot can later replace this comment, for example CLA Assistant, which records signatures against GitHub logins. The Maintainer won't merge a pull request until its author has signed.
