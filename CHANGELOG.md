# Changelog

Notable changes to pocketBrain. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/). Before 1.0, a minor version can change the brain format or the agent door; each such change is listed under **Changed** with what to do.

## [Unreleased]

### Before you update

- Your Gardener can now write a short opening paragraph on a page that ties its facts together (G713). It is off: each night the Gardener's wiki_sync only reports what it would write. Run `pocketbrain wiki opening on` when you want pages to take it, and `pocketbrain wiki opening off` to go back.
- The night pass can now notice a class you teach (G711). It is off: each night it only counts the classes it would ask you about. Add `teach: { ask: true }` to `rules/policy.yaml` when you want the question on your home.
- Upkeep now counts the held facts your assistants filed from receipts and other no-reply mail before this update, and changes nothing. Run `pocketbrain machine-mail` to see the count, and `pocketbrain machine-mail on` to set them aside, each with Undo on your home (G668).
- The phone app's Face ID now only makes the phone's own live sign-in fresh, and no longer counts its 30 days again: 30 days after pairing, the app asks for a new sign-in link to pair once more. Update the app (it ships over the air) with the hub, or Face ID answers "pair again" until it does (G642).
- Upkeep can now put a project page under the project it is part of (G640). It is off: each night it only counts the pages it would place. Run `pocketbrain wiki parents on` when you want them placed, and `pocketbrain wiki parents off` to go back to counting.
- From this update the nightly pass folds same-name project and org pages into one (docs/fold-twins.md). Run `pocketbrain fold` first to see what would fold and how many conflicts it would open, and `pocketbrain fold off` before the night if you'd rather it only counted (G635).

### What you see
- A person's page whose page holds no address can still show Last met when a guest's name on the invite is theirs; it says so ("Last met 2 Oct, by the name on the invite") (G746).
- Forgetting a person now also deletes your Ask chats that name them, and says how many; a chat is not brought back by the undo (audit v13, G763). The drill key for the Sunday restore test is added and removed as your own commit, on the hub only (G762).
- An assistant that says again a money-, health- or legal-shaped value it filed itself is no longer refused as a leak and cut to proposals only. The value is held for you to open, so it was hidden from the assistant that wrote it, and its own re-confirmation read as a guess at something hidden: you got a leak alert for nothing and its facts stopped filing until you cleared the hold. A value it wrote itself is now never a leak; another assistant's held value still is (audit v13, G758).
- A person's page shows Last met when their address is held under any email field, or written as a From line or in capitals (G739). Your own page names the latest twelve projects of an area that has more, instead of none (G740). The north star now reports how many people pages show Last met, how many past meetings tie no page, and how many live projects and decisions your page names.
- The Gardener's opening paragraph is checked part by part: each part must be said by one of the sentences it cites, with who it is about, so words from two sentences can't make a third that is false, and a "not" the page doesn't say is refused. An assistant that loses a sentence the opening rests on gets the page without the opening, and the opening lands as one commit, so undoing it takes the paragraph out of the page. It is still off until you run `pocketbrain wiki opening on` (audit v13; G760, G761).
- An assistant no longer reads one side of an open conflict as settled. Since packs gave each fact once, a value with a different one waiting beside it reached your assistants marked `[current]` and nothing else, and the week block stated it as plain fact. Now the fact's own line says `[current; a different value is waiting to be checked]` on every read, and the week's line says it too, until you settle it (audit v13, G757).
- Three smaller places now hide what the rest of the app hides (audit v13 slice audit): the Your week block of a pack withholds a value hidden from that assistant, as a read does (G764); a next step the calendar serves is scrubbed the same way when reads come from cells (G765); and Right now on your own page keeps a value you haven't opened yet behind a fresh sign-in, like your home does (G766).
- An assistant no longer learns the name of a page it can't see from another page's text. When a fact linked such a page, the assistant's fact list hid the link but the hub's own sentence said the name in plain words ("… is the founder at …"), and a Gardener summary or opening could say it too. The hub now writes it as a link, which each assistant's own read hides, and the name is withheld from text written before; the Gardener is refused a summary or opening that names it (audit v13; G754, G755, G756).
- Two values that differ in a name, a tense, a sign or a currency are no longer treated as the same. "Send it to Robert" and "Send it to Roberts", "expires" and "expired", -740 and 740, $740 and €740 used to merge without a word, the second one's source added to the first. Now they are two values: you are asked once, on Settle. A sentence said again with other capitals, punctuation or an article more is still one value (audit v13, G759).
- A person's area chip no longer repeats the org's initials beside a line that names the org, when the org is written in plain words rather than linked, or only the role names it, and whenever the line spells the area's short name, as "Option Voisins" spells OV (G736, G737).
- A person's page says a role that already names its org once ("Organiser of the North College board meeting", not "… at North College" after it), in its text and in the line under the name; its area chip shows the area's label, a short area in capitals, or nothing when it would only repeat the org; and it no longer says someone "also comes up in the sources of" a page the page already links (G729 to G732).
- Your own page now lists your live projects and the decisions that still stand, one sentence per area ("Your work projects: Atlas launch and Q1 roadmap."), so a project nothing else ties to is still one tap from your page. An assistant that can't see one of them doesn't get that sentence at all (G725, G726).
- A sentence typed in a page's box now answers right above the box, where you are looking, and the page says **Edited by you on <day>.** when it changed a fact, also when your box reads with a model; the same sentence sent twice is read once (G727, G728, G735).
- An assistant reading your pages no longer learns which of your other assistants filed a fact: it reads "An assistant's note" and `agent:[withheld]` where your own view keeps the name (G722, G723).
- Ask keeps chats: **New chat** starts one with its own context, **Earlier chats** lists them, and each stays across sign-ins and restarts until you delete it or leave it for 90 days. A follow-up about "she" or "it" stays inside its chat, however long ago (G716, G717).
- **Edit** on a page opens your own notes on it, and a sentence on a page that names no fact can be kept as a note with **Keep it as a note**; the page then says **Edited by you on 8 Oct.** (G718, G719).
- The question about a class you teach is on your home under **To answer**, with **Yes, I teach it**, **I'm taking it** and **No**, and it counts once in the number of things that wait for you. Once a page behind it is private, it asks without naming the class or the school (G715).
- A fact an assistant filed without a checked source now says an assistant told it under its sentence ("An assistant's note, 2026-11-02"), and a surname guess says "Guessed from the names"; a page reads "No source given" only when nobody is known (G720, G721).
- When you settle something on the settle screen, by a pick or by keeping both, the page's own text says it at once, not after the night run, so an assistant never reads the new value beside the old sentence (G707).

- The box takes sentences about you, with no model key: "I live in Toronto", "I prefer meetings after 10", "My plate is ABC 123", "Our rent is 1,850 a month" and "Remind me to renew the passport" go on your own page, and "My dentist is Dr Lam, next visit 12 Dec" ties Dr Lam to you and notes the visit, so "who is my dentist" finds them. A person's "number" is their phones. A new brain's first facts are on your home from day one (docs/owner-home.md, G708, G709).
- When a course code keeps coming up in pages filed from your mail, with people at one school behind the same mails, the brain asks you once: "You seem to teach PROD 20410 at North College. Right?" Nothing is filed before you answer. Yes files a page for the course under the school and your own word that you teach it, never touching your employer; the school's page then opens "You teach PROD 20410 here.", your page and the course page say it too, and the course page lists the pages about it. No is remembered, and that code is never asked again (docs/wiki.md "A class you teach", G711, G712).
- Ask answers the one thing you asked: "What's next for Atlas launch?" says its next step, not the whole page, and "When do we freeze the Atlas beta?" finds the step that says the freeze instead of "Nothing on that yet", with the page and its source under the answer (G706).
- Ask remembers who you were just asking about: after "who is Camille?", "is she my daughter?" is about Camille (docs/owner-home.md, G701).
- An org page opens with your own tie to it ("You work as a lecturer at North College."), from your own role and org, instead of listing you among the people there. The people there are said by how you know them when their page says it ("…: your colleagues."), and the pages whose sources name it are one sentence, not the same sentence once per page (G702, G703, G704).
- Open Settings on your home now lands on Settings' **Needs a look**, named as on the home and with the same number. Each thing it counted is there with its own taps: a proposal has Save it and Don't save, a name that fits two pages asks Same as … or Someone new, new pages held past their day have File them now, a rules change links History. What Settings shows but doesn't count is apart under **Also here**. The trust count ("52 need a look") left Settings, and the Brain's list of facts to check is now **To recheck** (G705).
- Your assistants get each fact once. A context pack used to give every fact twice, as a line and again as the hub's sentence; now it gives the line, and keeps only the sentences that add something, like who works where. When you ask an assistant about something it isn't allowed to see, the pack now tells it plainly that it isn't in its view, so it says so instead of guessing from unrelated notes (docs/hub.md, G699, G700).
- Your brain tests its own backup every Sunday. After the night pass, the hub reads the newest backup back out of your cloud folder, checks it is the same file it made, and restores it into a scratch folder to prove it opens into a working brain. Who knows and Settings say "Your brain was restored from Sunday's backup in your cloud folder in 40 s", or say plainly that the test failed and why. To let it open the real file rather than test the mechanics with a stand-in key, run `pocketbrain backup drill-key` once on the hub (docs/backup.md "The Sunday restore test", G690).
- When one of your assistants stops reading your brain, your home says so in one line ("Planner has not read your brain in 9 days"), linking to Who knows, which says when it last read and how often it used to. Each assistant is measured against its own habit, so one that reads weekly isn't flagged after a few days. Nothing shows while every connected assistant reads as it used to, and the line is never an ask (docs/owner-home.md, G697, G698).
- When one of the hub's scheduled jobs is late or failing, your home says so in one line under the count ("Your night pass has not run for 2 days"), and says nothing when all is well. It is never an ask and adds nothing to the count. Settings lists the jobs, doctor names each late one, the week report counts them, and `pocketbrain jobs` prints the whole ledger (docs/real-use-bar.md "One ledger for every job", G691, G692).
- A person's page says when you last met them, when you meet next and how many times so far this quarter, from the calendar you connected: "Last met 2 Oct · Next: Export sample walkthrough, 9 Oct". A meeting counts when one of their addresses is on its guest list or its title names them in full. Nothing is written to your brain and nothing asks you. The calendar's cache keeps guests only as hashes of their addresses, and now the past three months too (G694, G695). When the calendar knows a meeting newer than the last contact on the page, the page no longer also says the older day, and a meeting title that looks like health, money or legal shows only its day until you sign in again (G696).
- Pages now say how the pages they link are tied to each other. An org page says which of its people are on which project, a project page says where its people work, and a person's page says who else is on their projects and who else is at their org, each sentence linked and cited by the facts behind it (G685, G686, G687). Asking an assistant about a page by its exact title now brings that page first, ahead of a longer page whose title holds it (G693).
- Right now on your own page is your week. It lists what went past its due date, then each of the next seven days with its due dates, next steps that name their day, birthdays and calendar events, then what's coming up this month and the next steps with no date. Every page is a link and each line says where it came from. An assistant that asks context_pack about its week ("what's next this week") gets the same week as one dated block, built only from what it can see (docs/owner-home.md, G688, G689).
- Fewer cards ask you to pick between things that are both true. An org named in another case or with accents ("Lakeview college", "LAKEVIEW Collège") is the same org. The same long notice from two mails, its parts in another order, is one value. Two next steps from one mail both stay. Your own word is never asked against an assistant's guess. "Not sure, keep both" now sticks: the same two values never come back, and an assistant doesn't propose again what you turned down. Cards already on your home stay until you answer them once.
- What you or anyone wrote at the top of a page stays at the top. The hub's sentences from the page's facts follow it under "From its facts", where before they opened the page and pushed your paragraph down under "Earlier notes"; text already pushed down goes back up when it is word for word what was written there (G682, G683). Ask Braniac answers about a page with that paragraph and the page's facts beneath it. A mail id written into that text as "(gmail:…)" reads as one reference, not a broken one (G684).
- A link written into a page's own text now links back. When an earlier note on a project page links [[Kim Roux]], Kim's page says "See also Office move", cited by that note's sources, as a fact's link already did; only where everyone who reads Kim's page can read that note, and never on your own page (G670). A project page whose fact links another by name finds it among the pages every assistant reading it sees, so a same-name page outside one's view no longer stops it going under its parent (G671). The real brain's pages-ok count, after a deploy, covers the day up to the last nightly run, so a page changed since waits for the night that writes it (G672).
- The box takes "Paul believes …", "They say …", "I'm told …", "…, I'm told" and "Paul's email says …" as someone else's word, not yours. An assistant's fact telling it to pass, show, read, hand or publish something to anyone, or to all agents, is held like the other orders (G681).
- Your own page now reads like the front of a book about you. Under what it says about you come what's new, what you're on right now, who matters to you and how many things wait for you, each one a link (docs/owner-home.md).
- A line for a receipt or no-reply mail fact your brain set aside now says "Your brain set it aside today" under it, where it said "From today's email" though no email came that day (docs/owner-home.md, G673).
- Conflicts on one page from the same two sources are one card that names each field, instead of a card per field. Settle opens the first, and each answer goes on to the next until the last brings you home (docs/owner-home.md, G669).
- A receipt or other no-reply mail no longer becomes a page through an assistant reading your inbox. The hub already skipped such mail; now it remembers which mail it skipped, and an assistant's fact citing that mail is refused, whatever else it cites beside it (G679). Facts filed this way before are counted each night until you turn on setting them aside, each with Undo (docs/owner-home.md, G668).
- A person's page follows the workplace you name: when you say where someone works and their page sat under the org it named before, the page moves to the new org's area, with a line on your home and Undo. A page you filed somewhere else yourself stays put, and a source or an assistant never moves a page (docs/owner-home.md, G664).
- Pages, the home and Settings read as sentences: a role that starts like a verb is said as a role ("Hélène Lefebvre's role is retired teacher."), a new workplace reads "Maya works at Acme now", your own to-do on a page reads "You owe Ben the sample", Who sees names your assistants as names, Details say each fact and its date in words, and Filed for you says what was filed instead of a fact key (G663).
- You are asked to pick only when two values can't both be true. The same sentence in other words ("to retain" or "to be retained") is one value. Two mails from one sender on the same day update the value, and the earlier one is kept as history. A newer mail moves a next step along. Two different senders who disagree still ask. Developer notices from "…-noreply" addresses are no longer read for facts, and every conflict card says where its sides came from. Cards opened before this stay until you settle them; the nightly upkeep counts how many of them are false (docs/filing.md, G662).
- The box takes "Jane is the dean, says Paul", "… (says Paul)" and "In Paul's words, …" as Paul's word, not yours, as it already did "…, Paul says": it notes nothing and offers the fact as yours if you know it's so. What it still misses, and what it takes for hearsay that isn't, is listed in the guards' tables (docs/owner-home.md, G661).
- Each side on the settle screen shows what its source said, in quotes, so you pick with the evidence in front of you (G619).
- Ask Braniac knows your people by who they are to you: "who is my wife", "who are my kids" or "who is my boss" finds them from what their pages say they are to you, where it answered "Nothing on that yet". A word it can't place gets Ask's own answer, not the write box's "Try 'Ana works at Bay Institute'" (G649).
- The phone app no longer sits on its logo with no word while your hub is slow to answer. After 10 seconds it says "Still waiting for your hub" with Try again, and opens the page as soon as the hub answers. It reaches the app as an over-the-air update (docs/phone-app.md, G659).
- `pocketbrain dashboard phones log` also lists the app's calls the hub turned away before they reached it (a phone not on your list, another Tailscale login, an unknown address), each with its reason, so an "answered 403" in the app can be read against it (docs/phone-app.md, G658).
- A page you keep from an assistant by name also keeps its twin from it: a second page with the same name, kind and area, filed by an assistant that couldn't see the first. Before, the twin showed that assistant everything you had hidden (docs/fold-twins.md, G657).
- A handoff from one assistant to another no longer carries a money split, or any fact made of parts, that the receiving assistant can't see. The relay's check looked for hidden values only when they were plain text or a number, so a split's amount and who it's with went through and showed in the other assistant's What's new. It is now held for you like any hidden value, and a hidden page's other names are held too (docs/relay.md, G656).
- The phone app asks "Pair with <your hub>?" before it pairs from a link, and says when the link would replace the hub it is paired with; before, any link paired it at once (G647).
- After Show on another person's health, school, legal or money matter, the home no longer says "Your assistants get it now": such a value goes only to the resolver, and the line says so ("Only steward gets it: it's another person's private matter.", G645).
- Filing for you weighs a proposal that names its page against the pages its assistant can see, as the write does: a page by that name hidden from the assistant no longer makes it "held as a conflict" while a new page is made. When a check hands the hub's yes back, Settings says "left it for you", not "saved it", and that proposal waits for you instead of failing on every tick (G650).
- The phone app no longer keeps "Couldn't reach your hub. Is Tailscale on?" on every page after one Face ID try that didn't go through: the next page that loads clears it. It says that only when the hub didn't answer twice; any other answer is said with its number. `pocketbrain dashboard phones log` lists the app's last calls and what the hub answered (docs/phone-app.md, G651).
- A trial assistant can no longer test guesses at what it can't see. When a write carried a value hidden from it, it used to get a `leak_tripwire` refusal, while a write with a made-up value went to your inbox, so the refusal confirmed the guess. Both now get the inbox reply. You still get the alert, and the card says the write carried a hidden value (docs/add-an-agent.md, G648).
- Memory says which assistant runs each routine. The routines list has a section per assistant ("Town's routines"), the "These may do the same job" line names each routine with its assistant ("Town's Auto-inbox, and Muse's Proactive monitoring and alerts"), and a routine's page says "Town's routine" (docs/wiki.md, G641).
- Undo on a fold now undoes the fold. On your mind shows a fold as one line, "Two pages called X are one page now", where it showed a line per moved fact whose Undo took back one value and left the pages folded. Undo, there or with `pocketbrain fold undo`, also works after later writes: both pages come back and anything written since stays (docs/fold-twins.md, G644).
- A project that is part of another sits under it. "Lakeside cabin: check-in" goes under "Lakeside cabin (rental host)", and a page whose own fact names one other project goes under that one, on Memory, in the path above its page and in its parent's "Pages under this". Once you turn it on (`pocketbrain wiki parents on`), upkeep sets it overnight, only from what every assistant that sees the page can see, and the run's Undo takes it back for good; a name two pages share is left alone (G640).
- A sentence typed in the box about a fact with a private conflict waiting in the inbox asks you to sign in again and writes nothing, as it already did for a dispute; right after a sign-in it settles that conflict with your word instead of writing over it. Any other private value the box would note, such as someone's health, waits for a fresh sign-in the same way (G646).
- Ask answers on the phone again, and is now **Ask Braniac**. An answer given more than 10 minutes after you signed in, about a page that holds anything private, was hidden as soon as it was given, even the one saying to sign in again, so Ask showed only "Earlier answers here show again right after you sign in." It now shows, since it was written for that older sign-in and holds nothing private. An answer hidden later says "Sign in again to see this answer." in its place, with the tap to do it (G637). The tab, the title, the box and the opening line name Braniac, the brain's character; on a narrow phone the tab bar's words get a little smaller so all four tabs fit.
- Memory tells your notes from your assistants': their pages are **Assistants' routines**, the routines and jobs they run, with one line saying so at the top of the list; before, "Notes" and "Assistants' notes" sat side by side with nothing to tell them apart. A title written as a link ("[site](http://…) session reporting") reads as its words (G639).
- Routines that may do the same job are said. On Memory's routines list, "These may do the same job" names two or more assistants' routines that run on their own, read one source and do one kind of job ("both run on their own, read Gmail and send alerts"), and each routine's page lists the others. - Projects come in sections. A kind's list on Memory with pages in more than one area is grouped under each area's name, biggest first, so a project sits with the pages around it; "Cabin (rental host)" now gathers "Cabin: rebooking inquiry" and "Cabin: check-in" under it, and a page whose parent is set sits under that parent. Nothing is merged; each page still opens on its own (G638).
- The Gardener's org-describe run gets the $10 it was meant to have. `tend` wrote its budget as `"10"`, which Managed Agents reads as 10 cents, so the first run stopped after 14 searches. It now writes 1000 cents, and re-running `pocketbrain tend --managed --describe --apply` raises a kept file still at 10. `tend` also finds your door in `rules/gardener.yaml`'s `mcp_url` when `rules/hub.yaml` has no `public_url`, so `--url` isn't needed there (G636).
- Pulling down in the phone app loads the page again. Before, the spinner stayed up and the page didn't change. A label says when to let go. It reaches the app as an over-the-air update, with no new build (docs/phone-app.md).
- Things read as one thing. A held subscription on On your mind is one line, "Notes app subscription comes to CAD 6.20, is due 9 Nov 2026 and will renew.", not three ("…'s status is will renew."), and so is the line after you let it out; the card that counts them says how many pages they sit on ("7 new things on 3 pages only you can see"). An amount, a due day or a state says itself after the name everywhere on the home ("Harbour club is open."). A long note an assistant wrote, such as the Gardener's sensitivity review, is never read out on the home: the line says the page has a longer note, and its words stay on its page. Memory lists a name once: two pages with one name are one row ("2 pages with this name") that opens both, "Course, Fall 2026" and "Course: exam due…" sit under "Course", and three or more pages that start with one name ("Streamly plan", "Streamly offer"…) sit under that name. Nothing is merged; each page still opens on its own. The dots on Memory follow your own areas, biggest first in blue, pink and black, with a key above each list; before, any area other than work, teaching or personal showed an empty ring (G634).
- One project, one page. When an assistant filed a project into an area it can't see, each of its writes made a new page with the same name, so Memory listed it twice. The nightly pass now folds such a page into the older one with the same name, kind, area and level (projects and organisations only), picking no winner: where the two disagree, you settle it as any conflict. An open conflict on either page waits for you first. `pocketbrain fold off` makes it only count what would fold. `pocketbrain fold` shows what would fold, `--apply` folds now, and `pocketbrain fold undo <commit>` puts one back; for now that command is the only Undo, and On your mind doesn't show a fold yet. See docs/fold-twins.md (G635).

A `--write` that fails no longer cuts off a key the app already used, only the one it just made, and the bridge never follows a redirect, so its key stays on this computer. See docs/connect.md.
- `pocketbrain phone` pairs your phone in one command: it picks the phone from your Tailscale account, shows the lines it would set in `rules/` and writes them only on your yes, puts the dashboard on https with `tailscale serve`, restarts the hub, and prints a QR the iPhone camera opens in the pocketBrain app. Before, that was eight steps by hand. ## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-15

Never tagged: this section describes the first version as it stood on 2026-10-15; no version has been tagged yet: a tag is made only once CI on main passes.

The first version, used daily by one owner with hosted assistants connected over MCP. Not on npm yet: install with `git clone` and `bun link` (see [docs/quickstart.md](docs/quickstart.md)).

### The brain

- Plain Markdown notes with YAML facts in a git repo the owner keeps. Each fact carries its source, `as_of` and `review_after`; freshness (`fresh`, `due`, `stale`, `disputed`) is computed when a view is built and handed to agents with every fact ([docs/history/memory-shape.md](docs/history/memory-shape.md)).
- `pocketbrain init` starts a brain from nothing: a short interview, a plain-text seed, or a mail and calendar export ([docs/quickstart.md](docs/quickstart.md)). Notion import with text repairs.
- `rules/` holds the owner's policy: agents, slices, sensitivity, checks. Only the owner edits it, and any other change to it is flagged.

### Who sees what

- Carving: each agent gets a slice compiled from policy into its own cell. A withheld value inside a visible sentence stays withheld (`[withheld]`, `[[redacted]]`), and `explain <agent> [topic]` says why any fact is in or out.
- Per-agent revocable tokens, a pause switch, and an OAuth door for assistants that can only sign in (any standards-compliant authorization server; [docs/oauth.md](docs/oauth.md)).
- `pocketbrain add <agent> --slices …` onboards a new agent as a trial with money out; the dashboard can approve a new sign-in as a new agent ([docs/add-an-agent.md](docs/add-an-agent.md)).
- OpenFGA as an optional second lock on slices ([docs/fga.md](docs/fga.md)).
- Agent-to-agent mail goes through a relay that holds anything outside the recipient's slice.

### Agents' door

- One hub serving MCP (`/mcp`) and a JSON API (`/v1`, with OpenAPI), with an orientation generated per agent from its carve.
- Tools: `context_pack`, `search` (BM25 over the agent's own view), `read`, `remember`, `retire`, `capture`, `log_session`, `whats_new`, `memory_check`.
- The write path: schema, secret, injection and protected-path checks that can't be switched off, a leak tripwire, entity resolution, a conflict resolver that only takes a stronger or newer source, and an inbox for anything outside an agent's own area.
- Agent memory sync: readable agent memories are scanned and other agents quizzed; corrections are signed, approved by the owner and verified ([docs/memory-sync.md](docs/memory-sync.md)).

### Keeping it true

- Nightly upkeep and a sleep pass that forgets unused facts (undoable), consolidates agent journals and writes a morning note ([docs/sleep-and-explain.md](docs/sleep-and-explain.md)).
- A gardener role for a managed agent that reads everything, only proposes, works in set hours, and has a daily proposal cap ([docs/gardener.md](docs/gardener.md)).
- Checks after agent writes: a quick model conflict check and an optional second-opinion classifier ([docs/aftercheck.md](docs/aftercheck.md)).
- Source pulls: the hub reads the owner's own sources (mail, calendar, Discord, texts, Granola, Drive) on an allowlist in `rules/sources.yaml`, keeps facts with versioned refs and never stores the pulled text; a moved or deleted source turns its facts due ([docs/sources-pull.md](docs/sources-pull.md)). Live readers for Gmail, Calendar, Drive (listed folders only), Discord, texts and Granola are read-only, and nothing is read live without a `consent:` line in that file.
- Bring-your-own model for model-judged steps, with monthly and per-step spend caps; reads and writes are never metered ([docs/usage.md](docs/usage.md)).

### The owner

- A loopback-only dashboard: Needs you, who sees what, history, in-place edits, Ask, agents, spend.
- A daily digest, a text channel (iMessage, SMS or a relaying agent) with one-word replies, and quick capture by mail or a phone drop folder ([docs/text-channel.md](docs/text-channel.md), [docs/capture.md](docs/capture.md)).
- The first-week and real-use measures: `reexplain`, `minutes`, `week`, `fix`, `bar`, `task` ([docs/real-use-bar.md](docs/real-use-bar.md)).

### Running it

- `pocketbrain start` runs the server, dashboard, text channel, relay, nightly jobs and digest in one process, with launchd files for a Mac; a health probe checks the public door and the hub's own jobs.
- One hub and many replicas, with signed commits and a commit gate ([docs/hub.md](docs/hub.md)).
- Encrypted backups with rotation, restore onto a clean machine, and `pocketbrain drill` to prove it ([docs/backup.md](docs/backup.md)).
- `pocketbrain doctor` and `pocketbrain handover --to <dir>` to give the whole brain to a person.

### Known limits

- Install is from source; the npm package is prepared but not published.
- A hosted assistant keeps whatever it was shown in a session; pocketBrain decides what crosses the line, and can't reach across it afterwards.
- The hub runs on one machine the owner keeps on; agents can't reach the brain while it's off.
